# How a lesson works

Every lesson takes 10 to 20 minutes and follows the same pattern:

1. **Read** a short explanation with a small example.
2. **Answer** a few quick questions. A wrong answer explains why, and you can try again.
3. **Write code** in an exercise.
4. **Check** your code and read the results.
5. **Read more** in the official documentation, linked at the bottom of every lesson.

Nothing is locked. You can open any lesson at any time.
